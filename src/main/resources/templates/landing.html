Before you enter

Common questions.

A quick look at how StockFlow behaves in practice.

Can multiple warehouse locations be managed at once?

Yes. Every batch and stock balance is tied to a specific warehouse location, and transfers move stock between locations through a validated dispatch-and-receive step.

What happens if a delivery doesn't match the purchase order?

Goods receiving checks delivered quantities against the approved order line by line. Mismatches and rejected items are recorded before stock is confirmed into inventory.

Who can see and change inventory records?

Access follows five defined roles: Operations, Store, Finance, Customer Service and Systems, so each user only reaches the modules relevant to their responsibilities.

How is stock data stored?

StockFlow runs on a single connected MySQL database, keeping product, batch, location and movement records consistent across every module.

Can a stock movement be undone if it was recorded by mistake?

Confirmed movements aren't silently edited. Corrections go through adjustments and returns, which keep the original record and the correction both visible in the movement history.
